# Electromagnetics

## Lorentz Force & Magnetic Fields

Used in Magnetoplasmadynamic (MPD) and Hall Thrusters.

### Lorentz Force
Governs ion acceleration:
$$F = q(E + v \times B)$$

### Ohm's Law in Plasma (Generalized)
$$J = \sigma(E + v \times B) - \frac{\Omega}{B}(J \times B)$$
Where $\Omega$ is the Hall parameter.
