# Heat Transfer

## Spacecraft Thermal Balance

Thermal balance is governed by:
$$Q_{in} - Q_{out} = m c_p \frac{dT}{dt}$$

### Modes of Heat Transfer
1. **Conduction (Fourier's Law):**
   $$q = -k A \nabla T$$
2. **Radiation (Stefan-Boltzmann Law):**
   $$q_{rad} = \epsilon \sigma A (T_1^4 - T_2^4)$$
