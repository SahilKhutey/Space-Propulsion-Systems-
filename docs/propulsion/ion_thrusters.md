# Gridded Ion Thrusters (GIT)

## Theory of Operation
Ions are generated in a discharge chamber via electron bombardment. Two or three closely spaced electrostatic grids accelerate the ions out of the chamber.

## Performance Equations
- **Child-Langmuir Law (Grid Current Limit):**
  $$J = \frac{4\epsilon_0}{9} \sqrt{\frac{2q}{m}} \frac{V_a^{3/2}}{d^2}$$
