# Hall Effect Thrusters (HET)

## Theory of Operation
Hall thrusters use a radial magnetic field to trap electrons in a closed-loop E × B drift. Xenon propellent is injected and ionized by the trapped electrons. The resulting electric field accelerates the ions axially.

## Performance Equations
- **Thrust:**
  $$T = \frac{2 \eta P}{I_{sp} g_0}$$
- **Electron Drift Speed:**
  $$v_{Edrift} = \frac{E \times B}{B^2}$$
