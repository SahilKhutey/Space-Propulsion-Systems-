# VASIMR (Variable Specific Impulse Magnetoplasma Rocket)

## Theory of Operation
Uses helicon waves to ionize gas, and Ion Cyclotron Resonance Heating (ICRH) to accelerate the ions through a magnetic nozzle.

## Performance Equations
- **ICRH Ion Acceleration Frequency:**
  $$\omega_{ci} = \frac{q B}{m}$$
