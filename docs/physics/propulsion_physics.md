# Propulsion Physics

## Rocket Equation and Specific Impulse

Specific impulse ($I_{sp}$) is defined as:
$$I_{sp} = \frac{T}{\dot{m} g_0}$$

### Tsiolkovsky Rocket Equation
$$\Delta V = I_{sp} g_0 \ln\left( \frac{m_0}{m_f} \right)$$

### Jet Power
$$P_{jet} = \frac{1}{2} T v_e = \frac{T^2}{2 \dot{m}}$$
