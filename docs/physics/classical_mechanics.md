# Classical Mechanics

## Spacecraft Dynamics

Governed by Newton's Second Law:
$$\sum F = m \cdot a$$
$$\sum M_G = I \dot{\omega} + \omega \times (I \omega)$$

Where:
- $m$ is the time-varying mass of the vehicle ($m(t) = m_0 - \int \dot{m} dt$).
- $I$ is the spacecraft moment of inertia tensor.
- $\omega$ is the attitude angular velocity vector.
