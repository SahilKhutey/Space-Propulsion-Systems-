# Thermodynamics

## Propellant Expansion & Gas Dynamics

### Ideal Gas Law
$$P \cdot V = m \cdot R \cdot T$$

### Nozzle Flow & Thrust Coefficient
Ideal thrust for chemical/nuclear rockets:
$$F = \dot{m} v_e + (P_e - P_a) A_e$$
Where $P_e$ is the nozzle exit pressure, and $P_a$ is the ambient pressure.
