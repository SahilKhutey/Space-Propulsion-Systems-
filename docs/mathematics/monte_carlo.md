# Monte Carlo Methods

## Dispersion Analysis

Monte Carlo simulations propagate 10,000+ trajectories to determine spacecraft landing ellipse and orbital insertion dispersions.

### Inputs Dispersions
- Thrust magnitude: $\sigma_T = 1.5\%$
- Isp variability: $\sigma_{Isp} = 1.0\%$
- Pointing vector error: $\sigma_\theta = 0.05^\circ$

### Output Distributions
Computes the probability of collision or probability of entering safe orbits under nominal conditions.
