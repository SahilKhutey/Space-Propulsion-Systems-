# Magnetoplasmadynamic Thrusters (MPD)

## Theory of Operation
High current flowing from a central cathode to an outer anode creates a self-induced magnetic field. The Lorentz force accelerates the ionized gas.

## Performance Equations
- **Thrust:**
  $$T = \frac{\mu_0 J^2}{4\pi} \left( \ln\left(\frac{r_a}{r_c}\right) + \frac{3}{4} \right)$$
